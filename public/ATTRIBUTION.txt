hypertroph+ — data attributions & notices
==========================================

This application is an offline-first tool. It bundles only data that may be
freely redistributed. Restricted third-party datasets are never bundled or
distributed; when used, the user supplies them locally under their own licence.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
USDA FoodData Central
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Contains data from U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service.
FoodData Central. fdc.nal.usda.gov

FoodData Central data are in the public domain and are not copyrighted. They are
published under CC0 1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0). No permission is needed for their
use, but USDA requests that users list FoodData Central as the source of the data.

Bundled datasets:
  * Foundation Foods (release 2026-04-30)
  * SR Legacy (release 2018-04)
  * Survey Foods / FNDDS (2021-2023)

Licence: https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/api-guide.html

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Free Exercise DB
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Exercise catalogue (names, muscles, equipment) from Free Exercise DB
(https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db), released under The Unlicense
(unlicense.org) into the public domain. Bundled artifact: db/exercises.sql.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
hypertroph+ original data
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Seed/demo foods and exercises, muscle maps, and unit/prep taxonomies are original
works authored for this project (© hypertroph+). They are not derived from any
third-party database.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
